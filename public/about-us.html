OUR STORY

Why Heron AI?

The heron is a bird that stands still at the water's edge, watching closely, and moves only when it needs to, with precision. That's how our agent works. It watches your model quietly, speaks up when something needs attention, and acts only once you approve. "AI" reflects what sits under the hood, but the idea we care about is a calm, patient helper that never gets in your way.
Heron AI was founded by architects who were spending too many hours on repetitive modelling, cleanup, and coordination, work that pulled them away from the actual design. The tools were powerful but passive: they waited for every instruction and offered nothing back. We wanted a tool that could see what we were doing and take the dull, repeatable work off our plate, without ever taking control away from us. So we built one.
Our mission:
Take the repetitive modelling work off your plate
Catch problems while you design, not after
Make changes directly in your model, on your approval
Give back the hours lost to manual work
