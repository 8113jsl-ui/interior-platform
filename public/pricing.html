FAQs

Got questions?
We’ve got answers

Does Heron change my model without asking?
Later
No. Heron always shows you the edit first and waits for your approval. Nothing lands in your model until you say yes, and every edit can be undone.
How secure is my model and project data?
Later
We treat your model and project data as confidential. Heron only touches the parts you choose to share, and it uses them to run the agent and nothing else. Your inputs and outputs are handled under contract with our AI providers, and the Business and Enterprise plans add admin controls, SSO, and audit logs. Full detail is in our Privacy Policy.
Which design tools does Heron work in?
Later
Heron works inside Revit today, with Rhino, ArchiCAD, and SketchUp on the paid plans, and more on the way. It keeps your context as you move between them.
Can I try Heron before paying?
Later
Yes. The free plan lets you chat with Heron about one project at no cost. The paid plans come with a free trial, so you can test the in-tool agent and editing before you commit.
What's included in the free plan?
Later
The free plan gives you the Heron chat widget and Q&A about one project, so you can ask questions and get suggestions. The live in-tool agent, editing on approval, and the Dashboard are on the paid plans.
Can I change or cancel my plan anytime?
Later
Yes. You can move up, move down, or cancel whenever you like. Changes take effect at the end of your current billing period, and there are no lock-in contracts on the monthly plans.
