COOKIE POLICY

About this policy

This Cookie Policy explains how Bearplus Pty Ltd (Bearplus, we, us, our) uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit the Heron AI website at heronaiapp.com (the Site). It covers what these technologies are, why we use them, and how you can control them.

In some cases we may use cookies to collect personal information, or information that becomes personal information when combined with other information. Where that happens, we handle it in accordance with our Privacy Policy and the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

What are cookies?

Cookies are small data files placed on your computer or device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, or work more efficiently, and to provide reporting information.

Cookies set by the operator of a site (here, Bearplus) are called first-party cookies. Cookies set by anyone other than the operator are called third-party cookies. Third-party cookies enable features such as analytics to be provided through the Site.

Why do we use cookies?

We use both first- and third-party cookies. Some are needed for technical reasons so the Site can operate, which we call essential or strictly necessary cookies. Others help us understand how the Site is used so we can improve it. Third parties also set cookies through the Site for analytics and similar purposes.

How can I control cookies?

You decide whether to accept or reject cookies. You can set your preferences in the Cookie Consent Manager, which lets you accept or reject each category. Essential cookies cannot be rejected, as they are strictly necessary to provide the Site. If you reject other cookies you can still use the Site, though some functionality may be limited. You can also manage cookies through your browser settings.

Essential cookies

These cookies are strictly necessary to provide the services available through the Site and to use some of its features, such as access to secure areas.

  • heron_consent. Stores your cookie consent choices. First-party. Expires in 1 year.
  • csrf_token. Protects against cross-site request forgery and malicious activity. First-party. Expires in 30 days.
  • heron_session. Keeps you signed in to your account and dashboard. First-party. Expires at the end of your session.

Analytics and performance cookies

These cookies collect information used in aggregate to help us understand how the Site is used and how we can improve it.

  • _ga. Used by Google Analytics to distinguish users. Third-party. Expires in about 2 years.
  • _gid. Used by Google Analytics to distinguish users. Third-party. Expires in about 1 day.

Controlling cookies in your browser

The way you refuse cookies varies between browsers, so check your browser's help menu for details. Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge and Opera all let you manage cookies in their settings.

Other tracking technologies

Cookies are not the only way to recognise or track visitors. We may also use technologies such as web beacons (sometimes called tracking pixels or clear gifs). These are tiny graphics files that let us know when someone has visited the Site or opened an email from us, which helps us understand traffic patterns and measure the success of our email campaigns. Many of these technologies rely on cookies, so declining cookies will affect how they work.

Updates to this Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the cookies we use, or for other operational, legal or regulatory reasons. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
